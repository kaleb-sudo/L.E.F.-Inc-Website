Our Story

First in Tulsa.
Still the Best.

Founded by U.S. veteran Ron LaPalle, L.E.F., Inc. opened its doors in 1987 as Tulsa's first laser fabrication company. What started as a pioneering bet on emerging technology has grown into one of the most capable metal fabrication facilities in the region.

We've never outsourced a single process. Every capability — cutting, forming, machining, welding, finishing — lives under our roof and under our quality standards. That's not an accident. It's the only way to guarantee the precision and consistency our customers depend on.

Our customers range from startup manufacturers to some of the largest industrial operations in Oklahoma and beyond. What they all have in common: they found a vendor who actually delivers — on spec, on time, every time.
