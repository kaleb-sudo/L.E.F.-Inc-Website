Advanced Cutting

Beyond the
Flat Sheet.

L.E.F., Inc.'s 5-Axis laser cutting system simplifies the cut process of 3-dimensional parts. While 3-Axis lasers only work on flat sheet, bar, and plate — our 5-Axis system cuts and creates high-quality 3D geometry from formed parts and tube. The sophisticated laser head moves at high speed around the piece, making the necessary cuts around the formed part. We understand the design can sometimes mean we need to work with a different shape of material, and with more than 28 years of experience in 5-Axis/3D laser cutting, we are prepared to provide a solution.

Contour cutting and beveling on 3D formed parts and tube
359° C-axis rotation — virtually unlimited geometry access
G-code programming or proprietary laser manufacturer coding
