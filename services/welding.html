L.E.F., Inc. has a long-term focus on providing quality welding services, making an ongoing commitment to employ AWS-certified welders. Our highly trained, experienced, and skilled welders are able to perform a variety of specialized welding services. We strive to consistently provide superior quality welds that meet various industry standards. By utilizing our lasers, press brakes, and design team, we are able to manufacture our own weld fixtures onsite — using a fixture improves economy of production by allowing smooth operation and quick transition from part to part.
