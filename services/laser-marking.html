L.E.F., Inc. uses CO₂ and YAG lasers for product marking and engraving. Laser marking is virtually permanent because it directly interacts with the substrate material. There are a number of effects which can be produced on the work piece. Laser marking technology is a permanent and flexible way to identify parts — and can also be used to incorporate logos, serialized numbers, and barcodes. These lasers are also used in Aerospace, Medical, and Oil & Gas industries. Our tag and reel tracking program allows you to drop and drag your logo, track usage, and notifies you automatically when to reorder.
