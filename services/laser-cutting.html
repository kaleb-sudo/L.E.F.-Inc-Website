3-Axis Laser Cutting
Flat Sheet & Plate

The Fastest
Cut on the Floor.

Lasers are an extremely fast, non-contact cutting tool. Because the beam never touches the material physically, there is no tool wear, no contamination, and no force distortion. L.E.F., Inc. uses high-power laser systems to rapidly cut intricate designs and precise geometries — from simple shapes to complex nested programs — with tolerances held to ±.003".

Carbon, stainless, aluminum, brass, copper, and plastics in one shop
Non-contact cutting eliminates tool wear and contamination
Prototype runs or high-volume production — same precision, same lead time

5-Axis Laser Cutting
3D Tube & Formed Parts

Beyond the
Flat Sheet.

L.E.F., Inc.'s 5-Axis laser cutting system simplifies the cut process of 3-dimensional parts. While 3-Axis lasers only work on flat sheet, bar, and plate — our 5-Axis system cuts and creates high-quality 3D geometry from formed parts and tube. The sophisticated laser head moves at high speed around the piece, making the necessary cuts around the formed part. With more than 28 years of experience in 5-Axis/3D laser cutting, we are prepared to provide a solution.

Contour cutting and beveling on 3D formed parts and tube
359° C-axis rotation — virtually unlimited geometry access
G-code programming or proprietary laser manufacturer coding
