What We Do

Eight Capabilities.
One Roof.

From thin-gauge sheet metal to 2" plate — laser cutting, forming, machining, welding, and finishing all in-house. No vendor handoffs, no surprises.

Laser Cutting

3-Axis flat sheet and 5-Axis 3D cutting. Carbon, stainless, aluminum, brass, copper and plastics — from 24 ga sheet to 2" thick plate, any geometry.
Press Brake & Forming

Small and large format rolling, forming and bending. 440-ton brake with 20' bed, ±.0002 repeatability. Aerospace, industrial, and architectural.
CNC Machining

CNC and conventional: turning, milling, grinding, drilling, tapping. Steel, stainless, aluminum, copper, brass, plastic — short lead times.
Welding & Assembly

AWS-certified MIG, TIG, Flux Core, and Robotic welding. Carbon steel, stainless, and aluminum. Fully assembled, inspection-ready parts.
Laser Marking

CO₂ and YAG laser marking and etching. Logos, barcodes, serial numbers on carbon steel, stainless, aluminum, titanium, brass, and plastics.
Powder Coating

Electrostatic spray application with three-stage pretreatment. Parts up to 90"L × 50"W × 56"H. Full RAL color cross-referencing available.
Wet Paint

Industrial wet paint finishing: zinc primer, two-part epoxy, polyurethane, enamel, and multi-component coatings. RAL cross-referencing standard.
